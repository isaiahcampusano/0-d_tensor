THE WHOLE IDEA

One sentence to remember

“A tensor is numbers packed into nested boxes, and its dimension is how many boxes deep it goes.”

Technical vocabulary: dimension count is also called rank or ndim; each direction is an axis; the sizes of those directions make the shape.
